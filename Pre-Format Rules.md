1. ~~TWO players from each team are eligible to be kept for the following season.*~~
    
    An unlimited number of players are eligible to be kept for the following season so long as the the total salary is under an individual team's capital.
    
    - A player’s MAX tenure on a team is limited to FOUR CONSECUTIVE seasons before they are entered back into the draft.
        - **CLARIFICATION: A player can be considered "kept" for a maximum of four seasons.**
    - Players “kept” can be retained for the original draft price PLUS an additional fee of the LARGER between 10% and $5. Ensuing years will be between 10% and $7
    - Players drafted for <$10 and undrafted players prices will be rounded UP to $10.
2. Trades for ANY player CAN include cash considerations.
    
    - Trades DO NOT reset the players YEAR 1 draft price.
    - Cash considerations can be used in the following years draft
    - Cash considerations roll over to following years.
    - Vetoed trades CANNOT be resubmitted.
3. There are NO price restrictions on trades.
    
    - **CLARIFICATION: There is NO player that CANNOT be traded due to their salary.**
4. Any player traded CAN be kept.
    
5. The MAX allowance an owner CAN draft with is **$235**
    
    - 3a.) All money exceeding the $235 limit WILL be forfeited.
6. The Trade Deadline for ACCEPTED trades will be no later than the end of Week 12 (Sunday 6:00pm EST)
    
    - If necessary, the Commissioner will manually move accepted trades to the respective teams.
    - An accepted trade is defined as proposing and accepting a trade on the ~~ESPN~~ Yahoo app OR visual proof of confirmation (text message, email, etc.) verified by both parties.
7. Transactions and pending fees will be kept on the books. A team of professionals will overlook past transactions at season’s end to ensure all is accounted for.
    
8. KEEPERS ARE TO BE ANNOUNCED PUBLICLY TWO WEEKS BEFORE THE SCHEDULED DRAFT DATE(TBA).
    
9. **THE JEREMY BARBARA CLAUSE**:
    
    - ALL ROSTER WILL BE FROZEN AT THE END OF THE SEASON. NO ACQUISITIONS ARE ALLOWED DURING THE OFFSEASON.
10. ~~No draft dollars are to be awarded to those that make the playoffs.~~
    
    - There are no longer and draft dollar incentives for winning the league.
11. There is NO cap on the number of players in a given position.
    
12. Movement between divisions will be based on the _**regular season, intra-division record of teams.**_
    
13. There will be a free agency period leading up to the draft following the conclusion of the championship game that allows owners to trade draft dollars for a potential keeper. No give-backsies
